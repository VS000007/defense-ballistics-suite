Probabilistic Candidate-Site Optimization

A decision-support platform designed to solve the multi-facility defense siting dilemma. Given synthetic moving threat trajectories and candidate polygons within a 100 km × 100 km region, the system models geometric line-of-sight tracking, independent probability chains, and Mixed-Integer Programming (MILP) to deliver mathematically provable, cost-optimal site selection.
🔵 Academic Research Notice — Non-Operational Simulation

All threat vectors, candidate site polygons, speeds, altitudes, and costs are purely synthetic and projected onto an abstract 100 km × 100 km local grid. No operational defense parameters or weapon systems are modeled.

Geometric Line-of-Sight
Each selected site radiates a coverage cone based on its tier and altitude class. Tier-1 covers 35 km radius; Tier-2 covers 55 km; Tier-3 covers 80 km radius.
Probability Chain Model
P_i = 1 − ∏ⱼ (1 − pᵢⱼ)^zᵢⱼ. Independent probability chains across sites ensure cumulative interception probability is maximized per threat vector.
Diminishing Returns
Additional sites assigned to the same threat return diminishing marginal gains modeled via exponent α = 1.4, preventing over-allocation.

📘 Official User Manual & Operator Guide

Platform Overview

The Candidate-Site Spatial Optimization Platform is an academic benchmark tool for studying facility location problems and probabilistic coverage using Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP).

Navigation Guide

Spatial Deployment (GIS): Interactive map showing selected site coverage circles and threat vector paths. Coverage radii scale by tier.
Coverage & Performance: Probability chain analysis, geometric line-of-sight metrics, and per-site interception scores.
Decision Rationales: Explains why each site was selected or rejected with mathematical justification.
System Data & Inventories: Full database of all candidate sites, threat vectors, and arsenal entries.
Parametric Sensitivity: Charts showing how solution quality changes with budget, K limit, and model parameters.
Report Generator: Generates a full text/PDF-ready report of the optimization session.

Mathematical Formulation

Maximize: Σᵢ wᵢ · P_i(x)
Subject to: Σⱼ cⱼ · xⱼ ≤ Budget ($M)
           Σⱼ xⱼ ≤ K (site limit)
           P_i = 1 − ∏ⱼ (1 − pᵢⱼ)^zᵢⱼ
           xⱼ ∈ {0,1} (binary selection)
