📘 Official User Manual & Operator Guide

Platform Overview

The Candidate-Site Spatial Optimization Platform is an academic benchmark tool designed to study the mathematical problem of optimally placing defensive facilities on a discrete grid, subject to budget, coverage, and capacity constraints.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1 — Select Threat Profile: Choose from All 12 Vectors, or filter by Tier. Each tier represents a different flight altitude class.
Step 2 — Choose Policy Mode: Mode A maximizes the weighted defense score. Mode B minimizes total cost while meeting a minimum coverage threshold. Mode C selects exactly K sites.
Step 3 — Set Budget & Site Limit: Adjust the Budget ($M) slider and Site Limit (K) slider. The optimizer will respect these hard constraints.
Step 4 — Run & Analyze: Click Run Optimizer to compute the solution. Review selected sites, GIS map coverage circles, sensitivity analysis, and deficit threats.

Mathematical Formulation

Maximize: Σᵢ wᵢ · P_i(x)
Subject to: Σⱼ cⱼ · xⱼ ≤ Budget
           Σⱼ xⱼ ≤ K (site limit)
           P_i = 1 - ∏ⱼ (1 - pᵢⱼ)^zᵢⱼ
           xⱼ ∈ {0, 1} (binary selection)
